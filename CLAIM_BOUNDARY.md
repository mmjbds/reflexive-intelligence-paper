# Claim Boundary

The paper presents a formal research proposal and preliminary evidence under its documented model, task, training, and evaluation conditions.

It does not establish:

- General superiority of a 3B-parameter model over all larger models.
- Production readiness, safety certification, or customer deployment performance.
- General causal competence across all observer-participant environments.
- Replication outside the released materials and stated protocol.

Use the DOI record and paper text for the exact version, methods, evidence, and limitations.
