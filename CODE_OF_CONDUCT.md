# Code of Conduct

Critique claims, methods, equations, artifacts, and consequences rather than people. Be specific enough that another reader can test the point, disclose relevant uncertainty, and respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and publication boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, impersonation, de-anonymization, and deliberate misrepresentation are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, close, or remove material that violates these rules. Sensitive conduct reports use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
